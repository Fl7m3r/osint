SCOPE & ETHICSBY DESIGN

        

          
WHAT FLMP DOES

          FLMP handles public technical metadata and scans only targets you are authorized to test.
          
    
            
  • Server scans behind an explicit authorization confirmation — your own server or a signed engagement.
    • 
            
  • Public DNS, RDAP and Certificate Transparency for domains and IPs.
    • 
            
  • Locally decoded Discord Snowflakes, offline phone numbering plans, username format checks.
    • 
            
  • Every exposure finding comes with a concrete fix.
    • 
          

          
WHAT IT DELIBERATELY HAS NOT

          No breach/leak lookup, no stealer-log or credential dumps, no people search, and no account
          deanonymization (Discord/Roblox/TikTok identity, history, or cross-platform matching). Those
          target private individuals and are out of scope on purpose.
